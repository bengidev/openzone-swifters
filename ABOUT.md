# About OpenZone Swifters

## What it is
OpenZone Swifters is a **native iOS AI assistant** that brings AI models to mobile, helping users accomplish everyday work — writing, summarizing, planning, research, and quick answers — without leaving their phone.

## Vision
Make capable AI assistance feel native, fast, and private on iOS. Instead of a generic chatbot, OpenZone is **task-oriented**: it focuses on helping people *finish things*.

## Why "Swifters"
Built end-to-end with Apple's modern stack — **SwiftUI** for the interface and **SwiftData** for local-first persistence — by and for Swift developers who value native performance and platform integration.

## Core Principles
- **Native first** — full SwiftUI, no web wrappers.
- **Local-first data** — SwiftData stores history and context on-device.
- **Model-flexible** — pluggable AI provider layer for local (Core ML / Apple Intelligence) and remote models.
- **Privacy-aware** — keep sensitive data on the device whenever possible.
- **Safe by default** — Swift 6 with strict concurrency and strict memory safety enabled.

## Category
Classified as a **Productivity** app (`public.app-category.productivity`).

## Status
Early stage. Scaffolded from a SwiftUI + SwiftData base; AI integration layer in active design.
